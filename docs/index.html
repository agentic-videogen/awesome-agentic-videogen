THE SHARED PERSPECTIVE

One generative foundation.
Three ways to build around it.

The video model is the common ground.
The agent harness connects goals, tools,
memory, feedback, and execution.

01 / CREATE↗

Agentic Video
Generation

Plan the story. Coordinate assets and tools. Inspect and refine the final cut.

Brief → workflow → video—
02 / INTERACT↗

Agentic World
Modeling

Act in generated worlds. Maintain state and memory. Test what happens next.

Action → world → observation—
03 / IMPROVE↗
↻

RSI in
Video Generation

Improve data, models, evaluation, and the procedures that produce later updates.

Experience → update → capability—

Complementary research settings, not a hierarchy of capability levels.
